2. The service

Amtwise is free of charge. The app reads the text of a photographed or imported letter, replaces personal details with placeholders on the device and, with the help of an AI service, creates a plain-language explanation in the chosen language, including the deadlines, amounts and tasks named in the letter. The app can create reminders and calendar entries. The automatic replacement of personal details works as far as technically possible; under "See what was sent" you can check the text that was sent before relying on the result.
